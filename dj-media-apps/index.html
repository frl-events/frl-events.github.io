DJ & Media Applications

Play a set, photograph, film, or create art for a future event with FRL.

Application guidelines

  • Apply year-round. We can consider the same submission for multiple future dates.
  • Please submit each application type no more than once every six months. You can apply for both DJ and media in the same period.
  • Use links that our team can open without requesting access and that won’t expire.
  • Submitting doesn’t confirm a set, media access, or an art commission. We may only get in touch when we have an event in mind.

Apply to DJ

Submit a demo representative of what you plan to play. Tell us which events you’d like to play and include a sentence about why you want to DJ for us.

  • Club MAUL: Miami bass, Jersey club, techno, and house.
  • Internet Animal: Happy hardcore, gabber, hyperpop, and techno.

These are examples from past lineups. If you blend styles, send us a set that shows how you put them together.

If we reach out about a set, we’ll discuss pay, travel, and equipment before you confirm.
