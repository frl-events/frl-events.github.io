Volunteer with us

Club MAUL volunteer/community access program

Made possible by our community

As MAUL grows, so does the need for help with event setup and teardown. Our volunteer/community access program gives community members a way to get involved and help make each event happen.

Volunteering also creates an opportunity for community members who might otherwise be unable to attend.

Our community and volunteers coming together make the magic happen. Find out more about the program and apply to join our eligible volunteer pool below.
